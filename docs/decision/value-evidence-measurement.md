# Decision Change and Value Evidence Measurement

## Objective
Measure whether the service materially improved a decision and what happened afterward. Do not equate case counts, closure rates, logins, or recommendation acceptance with economic value.

## Minimum evaluation record
- Case, decision version, accountable decision-maker, and evaluation owner.
- Baseline / counterfactual: what likely would have happened without the service, its source, comparison period, and limitations.
- Decision delta: original option, final option, whether the service changed the choice or timing, and the human rationale.
- Execution record: actions actually completed, with dates and evidence.
- Observed outcome: operational and, where justified, financial result; measurement window and source.
- Attribution status: not established, plausible contribution with alternatives, or independently supported by a defensible comparison.
- Confounders / competing explanations, data quality, uncertainty, and reviewer.
- Decision-time targets frozen before execution; do not rewrite them after observing the result.

## Value signals
Prefer direct signals connected to a decision and verified outcome, such as:
- Time from qualified issue to executable decision.
- Verified cash exposure or cost avoided, with assumptions and evidence.
- Avoided delay or recovered commitment, only when the counterfactual is defensible.
- Scarce resource capacity released or reallocated, with the alternative use documented.
- Repeated issue recurrence reduced over a defined period, without asserting causality from association alone.
- Decision changed / not changed, including cases where review confirmed the original choice.

## Attribution safeguards
1. A changed decision is not itself proof of value.
2. A favorable outcome after an action is not proof the service caused it.
3. Separate estimate, realized observation, and causal attribution.
4. Include unsuccessful, neutral, and adverse outcomes; avoid survivorship bias.
5. Compare like contexts and disclose sample size, missing observations, and uncertainty.
6. Do not monetize ordinal 1–5 ratings or double-count the same avoided loss across cases.
7. Treat “no material decision change” as a valid result when review adds justified confidence or detects no better option.

## Prototype boundary
The MVP captures baseline, decision delta, observed outcome, measurement window, and attribution status in session memory only. It does not calculate ROI, establish causality, or persist an audit-grade evaluation.
